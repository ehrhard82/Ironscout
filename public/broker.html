Leads

Two kinds of lead. Permit holders are operators drilling right now (state permit filings, daily) — pick a state and county. Service companies and Trucking both come from DOT registrations (every outfit that runs trucks has one, with a phone and usually an email). Service companies haul their own gear to do oilfield work; trucking companies haul for hire. Use cargo and fleet size to cut thousands of names to the few hundred worth a call. Tick names and hand them to a salesman; each row has call, text, status and a note.
